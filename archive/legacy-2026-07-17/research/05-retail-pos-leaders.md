## Shopify POS

> **"Retail powered by your online store"**
> Founded: 2006, Ottawa | Public: NYSE: SHOP | Revenue: $7B+ | Merchants: 2M+

### Why Shopify POS Is Different From All Others

Shopify built e-commerce first and added POS second — the opposite of every other player. The result is the only POS where **online commerce is the primary system** and physical retail is an extension of it.

### Core Differentiators

#### Unified Online + Offline Commerce
- **One inventory** — sell online and in-store from the same stock
- **One customer profile** — customer who bought online is recognized in-store
- **One order history** — staff see online purchases when customer is in store
- Buy online, **pick up in store** (BOPIS) — order management flows to store staff
- Buy in-store, **ship to home** — sell items not in stock at location
- **Return anywhere** — buy online, return in store (and vice versa)

#### Shopify's Customer Context (The Magic)
When a customer comes in, staff can pull up their full history:
- All online orders
- In-store purchases
- Loyalty points balance
- Outstanding returns
- Preferred payment methods
- **Notes from previous interactions**
This is the holy grail of omnichannel — a complete customer picture regardless of channel.

#### Shopify POS App (iPad/iPhone)
- **Product search** with image, description, inventory count
- Barcode scan to add items
- **Draft orders** — start an order, hold it, continue later
- **Custom sale** — sell anything not in catalog with custom price
- Smart grid (customizable home screen shortcuts)
- **QR code scanning** for products (no barcode needed)

#### Shopify Markets (Multi-Currency, Multi-Language)
- Sell in multiple currencies and show local prices
- **Automatic currency conversion** at checkout
- Language localization for international stores
- **Duties and taxes** calculated for cross-border sales

#### Shopify Shipping
- Print shipping labels directly from POS
- **Discounted rates** with DHL, UPS, USPS, Canada Post
- **Fulfillment routing** — which location ships each order
- Return label generation for online orders

#### Shopify Payments
- **No transaction fees** when using Shopify Payments (their processor)
- **Shop Pay** — customer's payment info saved, checkout in one tap
- **Shop Pay Installments** (BNPL) — buy now pay later built in
- **Tap to Pay on iPhone** — no hardware needed

### Shopify's Wow Factors — What to Steal

#### Wow Factor #1: Shop Pay (The Payment Super-App)
Shop Pay is Shopify's payment credential stored by customers. Once a customer checks out with Shop Pay anywhere, their payment info is stored. The next time they check out (at any of Shopify's 2M+ merchants), it's **one tap to pay — card details pre-filled**. Conversion rate goes up 36% with Shop Pay.

#### Wow Factor #2: Unified Customer Profile
Every customer across all channels (online, in-store, social, marketplace) **is one record**. Staff see the full picture. This is incredibly rare and valuable.

#### Wow Factor #3: Inventory Splitting by Location
- Allocate inventory to specific locations
- **Transfer between locations** with receiving workflows
- **"Available to sell"** calculated per location
- **Demand forecasting** suggests how much to send where
- If one location runs out, **route orders to nearest in-stock location**

#### Wow Factor #4: Shopify Flow (No-Code Automation)
- Build workflows with trigger/condition/action rules
- Example: "When inventory drops below 5 units, send email to buyer and create draft PO"
- Example: "When a customer spends over $500, tag them as VIP and send thank-you email"
- **No code required** — 100+ pre-built templates
- This is the most advanced automation in any retail POS

#### Wow Factor #5: Analytics Dashboard
- **Sales by channel** breakdown (online vs. in-store vs. social)
- **Customer acquisition by channel** — where are new customers coming from?
- **Product performance** across all channels in one view
- **Returning customer rate** tracking
- **Average order value** trends
- Shopify's analytics engine is one of the strongest in commerce

#### Wow Factor #6: Shopify Marketplace (App Store)
- **8,000+ apps** in the Shopify App Store
- Every niche need has an app
- Loyalty, ERP, accounting, warehousing, marketing — all available
- **Shopify Plus** — enterprise plan with custom checkout and scripting

## Vend (Now Lightspeed Retail)

> Previously independent, acquired by Lightspeed in 2021 for $350M

### Legacy Innovations That Still Influence Retail POS

#### Vend's Offline Mode Architecture
Vend pioneered the **progressive web app POS** — it ran in a Chrome browser and worked offline via service workers. This architecture proved that cloud POS could be as reliable as local software.

#### Loyalty "No App Required"
Vend's loyalty program attached to **any email address or phone number** — customers don't need to download anything. This frictionless approach increased loyalty adoption rates dramatically.

## NCR Counterpoint

> **"Built for specialty retail"**
> Dominant in: garden centers, sporting goods, hunting/fishing, equestrian

### Core Differentiators

#### Deep Inventory Management
- **Lot tracking** — track inventory by lot number (critical for perishables)
- **Serial number tracking** — individual item tracking (firearms, electronics)
- **Unit of measure conversion** — sell by case or by unit
- **Vendor price books** — import supplier catalogs directly
- **Open to buy** planning — purchasing budget management by category

#### Specialty Retail Features
- **Layaway management** — take deposit, hold item, take final payment
- **Rentals management** — track rented items, due dates, damage
- **Work orders/repairs** — service items and track repair status
- **Club membership** — recurring billing for membership programs
- **Consignment** — track items from consignors, pay when sold

### Wow Factors to Steal
1. **Layaway** — critical for markets where credit cards aren't universal
2. **Rentals module** — track items rented out with due dates and deposits
3. **Work orders** — repair shop workflow built into retail POS
4. **Lot tracking** for perishables or batch-manufactured goods

## Cin7 Omni (Inventory Management + POS)

> **"Inventory-first commerce platform"**
> Founded: 2012, Auckland | Revenue: $50M+ ARR

### What Cin7 Does Best

Cin7 is first and foremost an **inventory management system** that grew into POS. For businesses that are primarily inventory-driven (wholesale, distribution, manufacturing), Cin7 provides capabilities no POS-first company can match.

### Core Differentiators

#### Multi-Channel Order Management
- **Single inventory** across: POS, B2C e-commerce, B2B e-commerce, wholesale, marketplace, EDI
- Amazon, eBay, Shopify, WooCommerce all connected to same inventory
- **3PL integration** — ship from third-party warehouses
- **Multi-warehouse** management with zone picking

#### B2B Commerce
- **B2B customer portal** — wholesale customers place their own orders online
- **Price tiers** by customer group (retail, wholesale, distributor, VIP)
- **Credit limits** — block orders when customer exceeds credit
- **Back-order management** — take orders for out-of-stock items, ship when available
- **EDI connectivity** — trade with big box retailers using standard data interchange

#### Production / Manufacturing
- **Bill of materials** — define what components make a product
- **Work orders** — manufacture to order
- **Batch production** — make X units, consume inventory components
- Finished goods flow back into inventory automatically

### Wow Factors to Steal
1. **B2B portal** — let wholesale customers order themselves
2. **Multi-warehouse zone picking** — optimize fulfillment efficiency
3. **Bill of materials** for made-to-order products
4. **EDI connectivity** for big retail supplier relationships

## Why Lightspeed Matters

Lightspeed is the most acquisitive POS company in history — they've bought Vend, ShopKeep, Upserve, Kounta, Ecwid, NuOrder, and more. The result is the most feature-complete POS platform for mid-market and enterprise merchants, covering both restaurant and retail with genuine depth in both.

## Lightspeed Restaurant — Deep Dive

### Core Strengths
Lightspeed Restaurant (formerly iKentoo in Europe, Kounta in Australia) is built for **complex hospitality operations** — fine dining, hotel F&B, large venues.

#### Table Management
- **Interactive floor plan** with real-time table status
- Color coding: available (green), seated (yellow), food ordered (orange), bill requested (red)
- **Multi-room/multi-floor** support — see entire venue at once
- Table merge and split
- **Reservation integration** — tables turn from reserved → occupied automatically
- Server section assignments with visual overlay

#### Order Management
- **Course management** — set courses, fire on demand or automatic
- **Item modifiers** with conditional logic (if ordered X, show Y modifier)
- Kitchen routing by station AND by course
- **Item 86 (out of stock)** — mark item unavailable, removed from all menus instantly
- **Order history** — trace any order back to who took it, when, what was changed

#### Lightspeed Delivery
- **Commission-free delivery** management
- Delivery zone management with time estimates
- Driver management with GPS tracking
- Customer order tracking page

#### Lightspeed Order Anywhere (QR)
- Customer scans QR → orders → pays → done
- **Zero server contact** option or **server-supervised** hybrid
- Menu images with rich descriptions
- Allergen and dietary flags
- **Custom branding** on QR ordering page

### Lightspeed Insights (Restaurant Analytics)
- **Labor efficiency reports** — revenue per labor hour
- **Server performance** — average check size by server
- **Menu performance** — gross profit per menu item
- **Occupancy tracking** — covers per hour, covers per server
- **Comparative reporting** — this week vs. last week, this year vs. last year
- Export to CSV/Excel for custom analysis

## Lightspeed Retail — Deep Dive

### Why Lightspeed Retail Wins in Complex Retail

Lightspeed Retail (formerly Vend, ShopKeep) excels at **complex product catalogs** — think sporting goods stores with 10,000 SKUs in dozens of variants, or bike shops with complex serialized inventory.

#### Inventory Management (Industry-Leading)
- **Matrix inventory** — size × color × material = individual SKUs, all managed from one product card
- **Serial number tracking** — track individual items by serial number
- **FIFO/LIFO/Average cost** accounting methods
- **Multi-location inventory** — see stock across all stores in real time
- **Inventory transfers** between locations with receiving workflows
- **Stock takes** (cycle counts) without closing the store — rolling counts
- **Shrinkage reporting** with categorized loss reasons
- **Supplier order management** — create POs, receive shipments, reconcile

#### Product Management
- **Composite products** — sell assembled kits from component inventory
- **Product bundles** — promotional packaging
- **Price books** — different prices for different customer groups or locations
- **Price rules** — bulk discounts, BOGO, percentage off category
- **Barcode printing** — print your own labels directly from POS
- **Product images** — visual catalog for staff reference

#### Customer Management
- **Customer profiles** with complete purchase history
- **Credit accounts** — charge-to-account for regular customers
- **Customer tags** for segmentation
- **Loyalty integration** with automatic point accrual
- **Customer-specific pricing** — wholesale vs. retail price per customer

#### Lightspeed eCom (Unified E-commerce)
- **Single inventory** for in-store and online — no separate systems
- Your online store syncs with physical store in real time
- **Click and collect** (buy online, pick up in store)
- **Ship from store** fulfillment
- Online store built on modern, SEO-optimized platform
- **Abandoned cart recovery** emails automatic

#### Lightspeed Payments
- Integrated payment processing
- **Tap to pay** on iPhone (no hardware required)
- Split payments (cash + card)
- Partial payments and layaway
- Multi-currency support

## Lightspeed's Wow Factors — What to Steal

### Wow Factor #1: NuOrder Integration (B2B Ordering)
Lightspeed acquired NuOrder — the wholesale ordering platform used by 100,000+ brands. Retailers using Lightspeed can now **order directly from brands inside their POS system**. The order placed in NuOrder becomes a purchase order in Lightspeed automatically. **This eliminates the buying office workflow entirely.**

### Wow Factor #2: Advanced Reporting Engine
Lightspeed's reporting is the most powerful in the mid-market:
- **Custom report builder** — drag fields, create any report you want
- **Dashboards** with customizable widgets
- **Benchmark reports** — how does your store compare to similar stores in your category?
- **Trend analysis** — machine learning-powered sales trend detection
- **Inventory forecasting** — predict when you'll run out of each SKU
- **Staff performance analytics** — revenue per hour, conversion rate, average sale by employee

### Wow Factor #3: Lightspeed Capital
Like Toast Capital and Square Capital — business financing based on transaction history:
- Loans from $5,000 to $500,000
- Repaid as % of daily sales
- No traditional credit check
- **Faster approval than any bank** — minutes not weeks

### Wow Factor #4: Hotel F&B Integration
Lightspeed integrates with major hotel PMS systems (Mews, Opera, Protel):
- Guests can **charge food and beverage to their hotel room**
- The charge appears on their folio at checkout
- **No cash or card needed in restaurants** for hotel guests
- Mini-bar charges routed through POS

### Wow Factor #5: Multiple Price Lists
A feature unique in depth:
- **Price by customer type** — retail customer vs. loyalty member vs. wholesale vs. employee
- **Price by time** — happy hour pricing automatic
- **Price by location** — airport location charges more than suburban location
- **Price by channel** — in-store price vs. online price vs. delivery price
- All managed from one menu with rules applied automatically

### Wow Factor #6: Offline Mode
Lightspeed's offline mode is comprehensive:
- Full POS functionality without internet
- **Automatic sync** when reconnected
- No data loss even in extended outages
- Works on both WiFi and cellular backup

### Wow Factor #7: Lightspeed Golf
A complete POS for golf courses — tee times, pro shop, F&B, lessons, cart management. Shows the depth of Lightspeed's vertical ambitions. They understand that "one size fits all" fails, so they build true verticals.

## Lightspeed Weaknesses (Attack Points)

1. **Complexity** — powerful but has a steep learning curve
2. **Price** — among the most expensive in the market
3. **Stitched together from acquisitions** — some products feel disconnected
4. **Support varies** — quality depends on which product line you're on
5. **Hardware agnostic but complicated** — you can use many hardware combos but setup isn't always smooth
6. **Mobile app** — less polished than Toast or Square

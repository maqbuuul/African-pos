# Square (Block) POS — Deep Dive

> **"Start selling in minutes"**
> Founded: 2009, San Francisco | Public: NYSE: SQ (Block) | Revenue: $21B+ (all products) | Merchants: 4M+

---

## Why Square Is the Benchmark for SMB POS

Square invented the modern mobile POS. Before Square, small businesses needed expensive terminal contracts, merchant accounts, and proprietary hardware. Square killed all of that with a $0 card reader and a free app. Today they've grown into the most complete SMB financial platform on earth — POS is just the entry point.

---

## Square's Product Universe

Square runs two POS verticals: **Square for Restaurants** and **Square for Retail**. Both share the same payments infrastructure, hardware, and financial services layer, but have vertical-specific features on top.

---

## Square for Restaurants

### Core Features
- **Free tier available** — actually functional, not a bait-and-switch
- Counter service, full service, bar, quick service all supported
- Drag-and-drop floor plan builder
- **Course firing** — send appetizers to kitchen, hold mains until appetizers are bumped
- Multi-location dashboard from single login
- **Auto-gratuity** rules by party size
- Void/comp tracking with manager PIN
- **Late seating protection** — close kitchen X minutes before close, POS enforces it

### Square KDS
- Works on any Android tablet (cost flexible)
- Color coding by ticket age
- **Multi-station routing** with customizable rules
- Summary view for expeditors
- Sound alerts configurable

### Square Online Ordering
- Free website builder included
- Online orders go directly to POS — no manual entry
- **Curbside pickup** with customer arrival notifications
- **Pre-orders** with scheduled pickup times
- Integration with Instagram and Facebook shops natively

### Square Handheld
- Works on Square-branded hardware or iPad
- Servers take orders at table, fire to kitchen instantly
- Split bills by seat or equally
- Process payments tableside (chip, tap, swipe)

## Square for Retail

### Core Features
- **Barcode scanning** — scan UPC barcodes to add products
- **Purchase orders** — send POs to suppliers directly from POS
- Vendor management with cost tracking
- **Stock alerts** — auto-alert when item below threshold
- **Item variants** — size, color, material with separate SKUs
- **Bundle creation** — sell items as kits
- Employee permissions by role
- **Customer profiles** with purchase history
- Exchange and return workflows with reason tracking
- **Multi-location inventory transfer** — move stock between locations
- Age verification for restricted items

### Square Inventory
- Real-time inventory across all channels (in-store, online, Square Online)
- **Shrinkage tracking** — record losses, theft, damage
- **Cost of goods tracking** — know your margin on every item
- CSV import/export for bulk product management
- **Low stock reports** with suggested reorder quantities

## Square's Financial Services Layer (The Real Moat)

This is what separates Square from pure POS companies:

### Square Capital (Now Square Loans)
- Business loans from $300 to $250,000
- **No application** — offers generated from transaction data
- Repaid as % of daily card sales (no fixed payment)
- Approval in minutes, funds next business day
- **No credit score required** — merchant's own sales history is the underwriting

### Square Checking
- Free business checking account
- **Instant transfers** from Square sales (vs. 1-2 day delay)
- Debit card for spending directly from sales
- No minimum balance, no monthly fees

### Square Savings
- FDIC-insured savings account
- Set aside % of each sale automatically (e.g., 5% for taxes)
- Separate pockets for different saving goals

### Square Payroll
- Full payroll including W-2 and 1099 processing
- **Automatic tip import** from POS (no manual entry)
- Tax filing included
- Same-day direct deposit

### Square Invoices
- Professional invoices for service businesses
- Online payment link embedded
- **Recurring invoices** for subscription services
- Partial payment support

## Square's Wow Factors — What to Steal

### Wow Factor #1: Instant Account Creation
Any business can start accepting payments in **under 5 minutes**:
1. Download app
2. Create account
3. Add bank details
4. Get free card reader by mail (or buy instantly at Walmart/Best Buy)
5. Start selling
**No contracts. No setup fees. No waiting for approval.** This is the single most disruptive thing Square ever did — and still does better than anyone.

### Wow Factor #2: Square Reader ($0)
The original magic trick. A free magstripe reader that turns any iPhone into a POS. The reader itself lost money but acquired millions of merchants. First-ever POS with consumer hardware economics.

### Wow Factor #3: Square Dashboard (Web + Mobile)
Real-time view of every metric from any browser or the Square app:
- Live sales ticker
- Sales vs. same period last week/year
- Best-selling items
- Busiest hours heatmap
- Employee comparison reports
- **One click to share a specific report** with your accountant

### Wow Factor #4: Square Marketing (Email + SMS Automation)
- Auto-capture customer emails at checkout
- **Automated campaigns**: first-time buyer, 30-day win-back, seasonal promotions
- Segment by purchase behavior (bought X but not Y)
- Text message marketing (SMS)
- **ROI tracking** — see exactly how much revenue a campaign generated
- Google Ads integration

### Wow Factor #5: Square Loyalty
- Points-based or visit-based programs
- Automatically attached to customer profile via card or phone number
- **Tiered loyalty** (Silver/Gold/Platinum)
- Birthday rewards
- Double points promotions
- **Loyalty ROI dashboard** — see how much loyal customers spend vs. new customers

### Wow Factor #6: Square Team Management
- **Free scheduling** for up to 5 employees
- Timeclock built into POS (no separate system)
- Overtime alerts
- **Labor cost in real time** alongside sales
- Employee can see their own schedule on the app

### Wow Factor #7: Square's Ecosystem / App Marketplace
- 1,000+ third-party apps integrate with Square
- **Single API** for everything (inventory, customers, orders, payments)
- Developers can build on Square in hours not weeks
- This means Square can stay lean and let partners solve niche problems

### Wow Factor #8: Square Appointments
- **Free scheduling for solo operators** (barbers, salons, consultants)
- Online booking page with real-time availability
- Customer reminder texts
- Calendar sync (Google Calendar, Outlook)
- Deposit requirements to reduce no-shows
- **Integrated POS** — after appointment, open tab and charge immediately

## Square Weaknesses (Attack Points)

1. **Jack of all trades, master of none** — wide but not deep in any vertical
2. **Account freezes** — Square can hold or terminate accounts with minimal notice (payment processor risk)
3. **Limited offline mode** — some features require internet
4. **Not built for large restaurants** — struggles with complex multi-course, multi-station fine dining
5. **Customer support** — automated and difficult to reach humans
6. **No enterprise-level features** — can't customize deeply for large chains
7. **Reporting is basic** — powerful for basics, limited for advanced analytics

## Biggest 2025-2026 Developments

### 1. Toast Goes Global + Launches Toast IQ
Toast launched **Toast Go 3** (April 2026) with global availability — first time Toast is officially expanding outside US. Toast IQ (conversational AI that executes) is now the product leader in restaurant AI. Toast also partnered with Coca-Cola for exclusive AI-powered beverage upsell within Toast IQ.

**Implication**: Toast is coming to international markets. African markets should expect Toast's sales push within 2-3 years.

### 2. Square Launches AI Voice Ordering + Bitcoin
In October 2025, Square made its biggest announcement in years:
- **AI Voice Ordering**: answers every phone call automatically
- **Bitcoin payments**: zero-fee BTC acceptance through end of 2026
- **External data AI**: blends weather, events, news with sales data
- **Square Drive-Thru** (May 2026): complete QSR drive-thru solution

**Implication**: Square is racing to be the platform of choice for the next generation of QSRs.

### 3. Revel Systems Acquired by Shift4 ($250M)
Revel is being wound down. Current Revel merchants transitioning to **SkyTab by Shift4**. New feature development on Revel has stopped.

**Implication**: ~18,000 enterprise restaurant merchants are actively evaluating new platforms. Window of opportunity.

### 4. SpotOn Launches Profit Assist (Industry First)
SpotOn became the first POS to embed **AI P&L analysis** (not just sales analysis). Ingests actual accounting data, detects anomalies, delivers specific savings recommendations.

**Implication**: The bar for restaurant analytics just moved from "sales reporting" to "full financial intelligence."

### 5. Lightspeed Launches AI + Competitive Benchmarking
Lightspeed AI (January 2026) with conversational intelligence. Plus daily-updated **competitive benchmarking** — compare your metrics against similar local businesses. Nordstrom extended NuORDER partnership in October 2025.

**Implication**: Competitive benchmarking is now a table-stakes feature for premium POS.

### 6. HungerRush OrderAI Hits 5M+ Orders
HungerRush's AI phone ordering has now processed 5M+ orders in production. Jet's Pizza (350+ locations) sees 92% success rate. Dairy Queen (via Presto) is deploying AI drive-thru across potentially 3,000 US/Canada locations.

**Implication**: AI ordering is proven at scale. Not experimental — operational.

### 7. PAX Technology Launches Android 16 Kiosk
PAX SK900 kiosk running Android 16. 16" and 21.5" sizes. 16,000+ apps in PAXSTORE marketplace. 17M connected terminals worldwide. PAX is the hardware infrastructure behind much of the world's restaurant POS.

**Implication**: For any POS in Africa — PAX hardware is likely the best cost/quality option for terminals and kiosks.

### 8. Oracle Launches GenAI Simphony Assistant
Oracle launched a generative AI assistant embedded in Simphony Cloud POS (March 2026). Available from any workstation. Handles login failures, device issues, system errors. Will roll out in 100+ languages within 12 months.

**Implication**: Even enterprise, conservative Oracle is going all-in on AI POS. This is no longer optional.

### 9. Shopify POS Kills POS Go (RIP Hardware)
Shopify retired POS Go in 2025, replaced by Tap to Pay on iPhone/Android (no hardware needed). Introduced POS Hub connectivity appliance. GMV lift of 8.9% measured when merchants move to unified Shopify commerce.

**Implication**: The trend toward software-only, hardware-optional POS is accelerating. Hardware is a means to an end.

### 10. Lavu Launches Marty AI (6-Agent System)
Lavu's Marty AI is the most sophisticated multi-agent AI deployed in a restaurant POS. 6 specialized agents covering: promotions, pricing, scheduling, waste, staff coaching, and nightly digest. Runs on iPad, Apple partnership.

**Implication**: Multi-agent AI architectures are the future of POS intelligence.

## Market Gaps — Nobody Is Doing This

### Gap 1: Africa Mobile Money
Despite mobile money dominating payment in SSA, zero global POS leaders have native M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, or Airtel Money integration. This is a $2B+ GMV opportunity sitting uncaptured.

### Gap 2: WhatsApp Ordering System
WhatsApp has 2.7 billion users. 90%+ messaging market share in many African countries. No POS system has built a native, structured WhatsApp ordering workflow. Every restaurant that takes WhatsApp orders does it manually.

### Gap 3: Tax Compliance for African Markets
KRA TIMS (Kenya), FIRS (Nigeria), EFRIS (Uganda), SARS (South Africa), GRA (Ghana) — all have specific ETR/ESD requirements. No global POS covers these. Local players are fragmented.

### Gap 4: Offline Mobile Money
M-Pesa and other mobile money systems can work offline in limited modes. No POS has built a system that queues mobile money payments during outages and processes when reconnected.

### Gap 5: Local Language AI
GPT-4 and Claude handle Swahili reasonably well. No POS has built a truly local-language conversational AI for African operators. "Niuambie mauzo yangu ya jana" ("Tell me yesterday's sales") should work.

### Gap 6: Chama/SACCO Integration
Millions of African business owners participate in rotating savings groups (Chamas, ROSCAs, SACCOs). No POS connects to these for business savings automation or group lending.

### Gap 7: Multi-Currency + Forex for Intra-African Trade
African merchants increasingly trade across borders (AFCFTA). No POS handles KSh/UGX/TZS/ETB pricing with real-time forex and multi-currency receipts as a first-class feature.

### Gap 8: Informal Market Features
POS systems are designed for formal businesses. Africa has millions of informal traders (market stalls, kiosks, mobile vendors) who are underserved. Features like "no barcode" inventory, "sell by count/weight without system," and "cash reconciliation by denomination" don't exist in global platforms.

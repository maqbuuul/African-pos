## TouchBistro

> **"Built by restaurateurs, for restaurateurs"**
> Founded: 2010, Toronto | Funding: $350M+ | Merchants: 29,000+

### What Makes TouchBistro Unique
TouchBistro was the first iPad-native restaurant POS — built in 2010 when iPad launched, it's been iOS-only ever since. This means it's deeply optimized for the Apple tablet experience.

### Core Differentiators

#### Local Processing (Hybrid Architecture)
TouchBistro runs a **local Mac mini server** in the venue. All POS data processes locally — so even with complete internet outage, everything works perfectly. Cloud sync runs in background. **Most reliable offline mode in the industry** because it doesn't depend on cloud at all for core functions.

#### iPad-First UX
- Designed for touch from day one
- **Swipe to navigate** between tables, menu categories, orders
- **Staff photos** on clock-in screen for easy identification
- Visual menu with item photos standard

#### TouchBistro Reservations
- Full reservation management
- **Waitlist with two-way SMS** — customer gets text, replies "here" when they arrive
- Floor plan view of reservation timeline
- Integration with Google, Facebook, Yelp for direct bookings

#### TouchBistro Loyalty
- Points or visit-based
- **Tiered rewards** with custom names and thresholds
- **Birthday rewards** automated
- Customer-facing loyalty display on POS
- **Integration with marketing** — auto-send email when points milestone hit

#### Menu Management
- **Menu item modifiers** with up to 99 options
- **Time-based menus** — breakfast menu auto-switches to lunch at 11am
- **Forced modifiers** — must select cooking temp before sending steak to kitchen
- **Menu photos** standard (not an add-on)
- **Descriptors** — allergen, dietary flags displayed to staff and customers

### Wow Factors to Steal
1. **Local server = true offline** — works even without internet for days
2. **Two-way SMS waitlist** — dramatically better guest experience
3. **Staff photos on clock-in** — reduces buddy punching
4. **Time-based automatic menu switching** — zero management effort

### Weaknesses
- iOS only — no Android support
- Local server adds hardware cost and complexity
- Less feature-rich on the financial services side vs. Toast/Square

## Revel Systems

> **"Enterprise Power. Small Business Simplicity."**
> Founded: 2010, San Francisco | Private (raised $145M+)

### What Revel Does Differently

Revel targets **multi-location enterprise** operators — QSR chains, franchise networks, large casual dining groups. It's significantly more configurable than Toast or Square.

### Core Differentiators

#### True Multi-Location Architecture
- **Central menu management** — update menu across 50 locations in one click
- **Location-level overrides** — keep global menu but allow local additions
- **Enterprise reporting** — compare any metric across any set of locations
- **Regional manager view** — see only your region's locations

#### Revel Open API
- **RESTful API** for everything
- Enterprise clients build custom integrations
- Connect to any loyalty, ERP, or marketing platform
- **Webhook support** — push events to external systems in real time
- White-label options for large franchise groups

#### Self-Service Kiosk
- **Highly customizable** kiosk UI (unlike Toast's more locked-down version)
- Upsell prompts with images at each step
- Loyalty login at kiosk
- **Accessibility compliance** (ADA)
- Multiple payment methods including cash (with change dispenser)

#### Kitchen Production System
- **Production batching** — make 10 burgers at once, assign to multiple tickets
- **Rush order flagging** — mark ticket as rush, appears at top of KDS
- **Recipe display** on KDS — chef sees recipe for complex items
- **Prep time management** — alerts for items that take longer

#### Revel's Drive-Thru Mode
- Dedicated drive-thru order queue
- **Vehicle tracking** — time in queue per car
- **Pre-sets for regulars** — returning customer's usual order available with one tap
- Integration with drive-thru speaker systems

### Wow Factors to Steal
1. **Enterprise menu inheritance** — global menu with local overrides
2. **Production batching on KDS** — batch cooking for high volume
3. **Drive-thru vehicle tracking** with time-in-queue analytics
4. **Recipe display on KDS** — kitchen staff training built in

## Oracle MICROS Simphony

> The enterprise POS for hotel chains, stadiums, and large restaurant groups

### What MICROS Dominates
Oracle MICROS runs the POS for **Marriott, Hilton, McDonald's, Hard Rock, MGM Resorts**, and thousands of other enterprise hospitality operations. It is far and away the most installed enterprise restaurant POS by volume at large chains.

### Core Differentiators

#### Hotel PMS Integration (Unmatched)
- Deep native integration with Oracle Opera (dominant hotel PMS)
- **Room charge routing** — F&B charge goes to guest folio instantly
- **Meeting room billing** — group charges to master folio
- Revenue center management across all hotel outlets
- **Night audit** automation

#### Stadium & Venue Operations
- **Concession stand mode** — ultra-fast, high-volume ordering
- **Portable ordering** — seat service with handhelds for sections
- **Cashless venue** support
- Inventory management for high-volume temporary events
- **Combo meal builder** optimized for sports concessions

#### Enterprise Configuration
- Configure **thousands of menu items** across hundreds of locations
- **Workstation profiles** — same terminal behaves differently based on role
- **Day part pricing** — automatic price changes at set times
- **Enterprise message center** — send notes to all locations

#### MICROS Reporting (Finest Detail)
- Revenue center analysis (bar vs. kitchen vs. patio)
- Employee efficiency metrics
- **Void and comp analysis** — detailed tracking with manager override audit
- **Speed of service reports** — order to payment time
- Custom Crystal Reports for any metric

### Wow Factors to Steal
1. **Revenue center management** — analyze each profit center separately
2. **Void/comp audit trail** — every exception tracked with manager approval
3. **Stadium seat service mode** — ordering brought to the guest
4. **Day-part price automation** with complex rule sets

### Weaknesses
- Extremely expensive (enterprise licensing)
- Requires professional implementation (months, not hours)
- Notoriously poor UX — designed for power users not casual operators
- Oracle's bureaucratic sales process
- Not available for small operators at any reasonable price
